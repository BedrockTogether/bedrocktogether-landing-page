# How to Join a Minecraft Server on Nintendo Switch

The Switch is the awkward one. **Minecraft on Nintendo Switch does not show LAN games**,
so the method that works on Xbox and PlayStation cannot work here. Instead,
BedrockTogether has a second method: it broadcasts your server over Xbox Live so that it
shows up in Minecraft's in-game **Friends** tab, which the Switch *does* read.

This is the **Switch · Friends** tab in the app, and it is still marked **BETA**.

## Read this before you start

> **Use a separate Microsoft account. Not your main one.**
>
> Broadcasting to Xbox Live works by emulating a game client. This may break Xbox
> Live's terms of service, and **the account you sign in with could be banned.** Sign
> in with a different Microsoft / Xbox account than the one you play Minecraft on.
>
> This is the same warning the app shows you before sign-in. We would rather you read
> it twice than lose an account you care about.

If that trade-off is not acceptable to you, do not use this method. On Xbox and
PlayStation the [LAN method](/how-to-join-minecraft-server-xbox/) carries no such
caveat, and you should prefer it there.

## What you need

* A Nintendo Switch, Switch Lite or Switch OLED with Minecraft Bedrock Edition
* An iPhone, iPad or Android phone with BedrockTogether installed
* **A throwaway Microsoft account** with an Xbox profile (gamertag) on it
* The server's IP address and port

## Step by step

1. **Open BedrockTogether** and switch to the **Switch · Friends** tab.
2. **Enter the server IP and port** you want to play on.
3. **Tap "Sign in and Broadcast".** Read the warning, then continue.
4. **Sign in with your separate Microsoft account.** The app shows a code and opens the
   Microsoft sign-in page with the code already filled in. Sign in there, then come
   back to the app.
5. **If you are told the account has no gamertag:** open `xbox.com`, sign in with that
   same account, pick a gamertag, then return to the app and broadcast again.
6. **Tap "Broadcast to Friends".**
7. **On your Switch, open Minecraft** → **Play** → the **Friends** tab. The broadcast
   account's game appears there. Join it and you will be transferred to the server.

Tap **Stop Broadcast** in the app when you are finished.

## Notes and limits

* **The account must be a friend of the account you play on.** Add the broadcast
  gamertag as a friend from your Switch first, otherwise its game will not be listed.
* **This method is not Switch-only.** Because it goes through Xbox Live rather than the
  local network, it also works when the LAN method fails on Xbox or PlayStation — for
  example on a network you do not control, like a dorm or hotel Wi-Fi.
* **It is beta.** Broadcasting is occasionally unavailable; the app will tell you when
  that happens.
* **If the account does get banned,** do not reuse it. Sign out and use another
  separate account.

## Frequently asked

**Is there a way to do this without an extra account?** Not on Switch. The console gives
no LAN listing and no server address field, so Xbox Live is the only route in.

**Will my main account get banned for joining?** The warning is about the account doing
the *broadcasting*, which is the one running inside the app. That is why it should never
be your main account.

**Does the Switch need to be on the same Wi-Fi?** No. This method does not use the local
network at all.
