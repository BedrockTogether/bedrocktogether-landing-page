How to join a Minecraft server on your console

Minecraft Bedrock Edition gives consoles no way to type in a server address. BedrockTogether runs on your phone and puts any Bedrock server in front of your console — no PC, no DNS rerouting, no router settings. Pick your console to get the steps.

Xbox

Xbox One, Series S and Series X. The server shows up as a LAN game in the Worlds tab.

PlayStation

PS4 and PS5. Same LAN method, same three steps, no DNS changes needed.

Nintendo Switch

Switch can't see LAN games, so it needs the Xbox Live broadcast method instead.
